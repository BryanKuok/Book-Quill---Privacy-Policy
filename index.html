Book & Quill Privacy Policy

Last updated Nov 1, 2026

Summary

Book & Quill collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no Book & Quill server. Everything you create stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account.

The developer of Book & Quill never receives any of this information.

Your library and iCloud

Your reading lists, entries, notes, templates, banners and the covers you add yourself are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they sync through your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit.

That database belongs to your Apple Account. The developer cannot read it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Deleting an entry or list removes it from all your synced devices. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device; your iCloud copy stays until you delete it in Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

Google Books cover search

Cover search is optional and off until you add your own Google Books API key. The key belongs to your Google Cloud project, not to Book & Quill.

  • Your key is kept in your iCloud Keychain so it works on all your devices. If iCloud Keychain is unavailable, it stays on that device only.
  • What is sent to Google: the search words (usually a book's title and author) and your key. Requests go directly from your device to Google; Book & Quill has no server in between.
  • Covers from Google Books are not stored by Book & Quill. The app keeps a link to Google's image and loads it from Google when the cover is shown. Your device may cache it briefly, only as long as Google's servers allow.
  • Covers you add yourself (photos, files, image links you paste) are resized and stored with the entry in your library.

Google's use of these requests is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google APIs Terms of Service.

Book lookup and import

When you use Find Book or search for a cover without a Google Books key, the title, author or ISBN you type or scan is sent directly from your device to Open Library, a project of the Internet Archive, to look up the book. No key or account is involved. Covers found there are shown from Open Library and not saved by Book & Quill.

When you import a library, the CSV file you choose is read on your device and its books are added to a new list in your iCloud. The file itself isn't kept or sent anywhere.

Museum art banners

Banner art comes from the public-domain collections of the Art Institute of Chicago and The Metropolitan Museum of Art. When Book & Quill picks art for a new list, or you search for a banner, your search words go directly from your device to those museums. No key or account is involved.

A banner you choose is saved with the list, and its credit line (artist, title, museum) is shown on the banner. The museums' handling of requests is covered by their own privacy policies.

Apple Intelligence

On supported devices, Book & Quill uses Apple's on-device foundation model to help write cover searches, draft templates and similar tasks. These requests are processed on your device and are not sent to the developer or any third party. If Apple Intelligence is off or unavailable, those features are simply hidden.

Apple Music

You can give a list or an entry a reading soundtrack from Apple Music. Book & Quill asks for access to Media & Apple Music the first time, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

With Apple Intelligence on, Book & Quill can suggest a soundtrack for a book, from Apple Music and your own library. The names, artists and genres of your albums and playlists, together with the book's title, author and type, are given to Apple's on-device model to choose from. Your library never leaves your device. The model also suggests a few Apple Music searches (moods, styles or composers that suit the book), which Book & Quill sends to Apple Music like any search you type.

  • Searches and playback go directly between your device and Apple through MusicKit; Book & Quill never sees your Apple Account or listening history.
  • The soundtrack you choose (its name, artist and a link) is saved with the list or entry and syncs through your iCloud.
  • Apple's handling of Apple Music data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Photos and camera

Book & Quill asks to use the camera only when you choose to take a cover photo. When you pick from your photo library, the system picker gives Book & Quill only the image you choose; the app never sees the rest of your library.

Children

Book & Quill collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Book & Quill will never start collecting personal data without saying so in the app first.

Contact and cover removal

Questions about this policy, or a request to remove content, go to bryankuok@icloud.com.

If you hold the rights to a book cover or artwork shown in Book & Quill and want it removed, email the title and what you would like changed. Covers from Google Books are links to Google's servers, so a removal there is also handled by Google's content removal process.
